About

I am an incoming Biomedical Engineering student at the National University of Singapore. My interests sit at the intersection of engineering and neuroscience, particularly brain-machine interfaces, neural technologies, and computational neuroscience.

I am interested in research that turns signals from the nervous system into useful information, tools, and technologies — from neural data analysis to interfaces that connect biology with machines.
