About

I am an incoming Biomedical Engineering student at the National University of Singapore, with a growing interest in neuroengineering and research.

I am particularly drawn to technologies that interface with the nervous system: neural sensing, brain-machine interfaces, bioelectronics, and computational methods for understanding biological signals.

Before university, I am looking to deepen my research experience and build the technical foundations needed to work seriously in this field.

Selected research

HCII 2024 Nanyang Research Programme · Gold Award

Deep learning for digital clock drawing analysis in mild cognitive impairment detection.

An exploratory research project investigating how deep learning and digital clock-drawing data on consumer platforms could support the detection of mild cognitive impairment.

The work was conducted through the Nanyang Research Programme and subsequently published at Human-Computer Interaction International 2024.
